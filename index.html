Our Heritage & Craft

Three Generations of Slow Baking.

It began with our grandfather's wood-fired stone oven. While early morning fog settled over the streets, our family mixed flour, unrefined sugars, and fresh churned pasture butter by hand.

DeeDee's Bakery remains rooted in those timeless techniques: no artificial stabilizers, slow-fermentation doughs, and cakes baked to order each morning. In 2024, we extended our artisan devotion to slow-churned ice cream custards—giving our patrons a sweet balance of warmth and chill under one roof.
