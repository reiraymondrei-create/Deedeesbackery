About Us

My story began with my Grandfather’s kitchen notes. He loved the rhythm of sourdough starters and the smell of toasted hearth loaves at dawn. I was founded on the corner of Elm and Maple in 2004, and we have remained committed to pure ingredients: stone-milled unbleached flour, cultured butter, and eggs straight from the farm.

In the early days, deep hot morning tea would meet sweet golden biscuits. We folded every dough with pastry scrapers and baked slowly until each loaf rang hollow with golden crust.

We source fresh cream, whole Madagascar vanilla pods, organic strawberries from the valley, and single-origin chocolates. Slow-churning ice cream has kept our parlor a neighborhood fixture through every sweet season.

Whether celebrating a wedding milestone or grabbing a midday cinnamon bun, our bakery welcomes you with warmth, heritage, and genuine craft.
